What to Know Before You Book

Booking

How to Book a Stripper in Charleston

Private strippers in Charleston require advance booking — typically 24 to 72 hours minimum. During peak bachelorette season (March through October) book at least one to two weeks ahead. A deposit is standard to secure your date and time.

Pricing

How Much Do Strippers Cost in Charleston?

Private stripper rates in Charleston SC typically range from $150 to $400+ depending on performer, show length, travel distance, and event type. Club entertainment varies by venue with cover charges, VIP table minimums, and tip expectations on top of entry.

Venues

Where Can a Private Show Take Place?

Private strippers serving Charleston perform at Airbnbs, hotel suites, private homes, vacation rentals, event venues, and private party spaces. Folly Beach rentals, downtown Charleston hotels, and Mount Pleasant vacation homes are all common locations.
